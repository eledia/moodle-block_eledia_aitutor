# User guide

The eLeDia.ai Tutor is an AI assistant embedded in Moodle. Depending on how your
site configures it, it appears either directly inside a block or behind an
**Open the eLeDia.ai Tutor** button (as a docked panel, a modal dialog, or a
full-screen experience).

## Chatting

- Type your question and press **Enter** to send (use **Shift+Enter** for a new
  line).
- The tutor's answer appears as a formatted message. If the tutor cites sources,
  they are listed beneath the answer.
- Use the **copy** button on an answer to copy its text.
- If a message fails, press **Retry**.

## Course vs. general questions

- On a course page the tutor can answer course-specific questions such as
  *"What do I need to do for this week's assignment?"*.
- Anywhere, you can ask general questions such as *"Which courses am I enrolled
  in?"*.

The tutor only ever sees what **you** are allowed to see in Moodle — it acts
with your permissions.

## History

If history is enabled, the clock icon opens your saved conversations. Click one
to reload it, or use the trash icon to delete it. Deleting removes Moodle's
pointer to the conversation; ask your administrator about the RAG server's own
retention policy.

## Accessibility

- Fully keyboard operable; **Esc** closes the modal/full-screen panel.
- New answers are announced to screen readers via an ARIA live region.
- Honours your *reduced motion* preference.
